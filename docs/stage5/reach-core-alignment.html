1 · Deleting

When can something be deleted for good?

Stage 4 said (Q17 = B)

Everyone archives stock. A Manager or Admin can delete stock for good, at any time. Admins can delete people for good.

REACH Core says (R4, Q38)

Archive, don't delete. Deleting is only for a mistake, within 24 hours of creating it, by Manage level, and logged. Patient files and the change log are never deleted.

What changes here

Archive stays for everyone. “Delete for good” only appears for 24 hours after something was created, for Manage level. After that, only Archive.

2 · Question Q20

Staff / Manager / Admin, or View / Work / Manage?

Stage 4 said

Three roles inside this app: Staff, Manager, Admin (as in June). Several answers use them: Q17 (delete), Q19 (roster), B14 (last Admin).

REACH Core says (R3)

One account per person. Access to an app comes from their office. Inside each app a person has one of three levels: View, Work, Manage. REACH's own note for this app: “Staff/Manager/Admin is not the shape to copy.”

What needs deciding

How today's three roles map onto View / Work / Manage. The table shows what each answer means for who can do what.

3 · People list

Whose list of people is it, for now?

Stage 4 said (F2.1, F14.1)

People come from the one REACH people list, shared with EDP.

REACH says (Q37, Q18)

There's no shared list yet. Each app keeps its own list in the same “Core shape” (hidden ID, name, office, stamps). Ops Management becomes the keeper later and merges them. Your words: “Ops Management is, in my head, where People lists are stored.”

What changes here

Staff keeps its own list now, in the Core shape, so it can be merged without retyping. The screens stay as in batch 4. When Ops Management exists, this app reads the list from there.

4 · Dates and times

Two kinds of time, two rules

Stage 4 said (ALL.4)

“No date is ever written or read in world time (UTC).” Written against June bug B13.

REACH Core says (R6)

“Times are stored in UTC and shown in local time.” Standard in aviation, and needed so EDP and this app agree on when something happened.

Both are right, for different things

A moment (when Anna dispensed) is stored in UTC and shown in base time. A calendar date (an expiry, a course done) is stored as a plain date with no time, so it can never slip a day. I'd reword ALL.4 to say exactly this.

A moment
stored: 2026-11-27 03:42 UTC shown: 27 Nov 2026, 13:42 (Port Moresby) 26 Nov 2026, 22:42 (Lima)
A calendar date
stored: 2027-03-31 (no time at all) shown: 31 Mar 2027 — in every country June bug B13: stored as a moment → “Expired” a day early

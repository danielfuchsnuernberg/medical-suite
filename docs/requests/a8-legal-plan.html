REACH Medical Suite · A8 · privacy and legal plan · 28 Nov 2026

What REACH has to get right before real people use the program

You asked for a plan of the privacy and legal duties: staff data, the controlled-drug register, patient files later, Papua New Guinea and Peru, Europe and the United States (added 28 Nov: the strictest laws, as the yardstick), how the program is handed out, and the free software inside it. This page sorts each duty by who has to decide it. I checked public sources on 28 Nov 2026; anything marked to confirm I could not settle from them.

This is not legal advice. It is a checklist to take to a lawyer and to REACH leadership. Laws in both countries are changing (PNG has a data protection bill in consultation in 2026). A lawyer in each country has to confirm the purple items before release 1 goes to the nurses.

1. What the program holds, and how sensitive it is

Low

Stock

  • items, amounts, lots, expiry
  • places (Store, bags)
Not personal. Business data of REACH.
Personal

Staff

  • names, email, job, office
  • training, certificates, dates
  • medical code (doctors)
  • password and PIN (stored scrambled)
Personal data of real staff.
Regulated

Controlled-drug register

  • who took what, when, why
  • witness, approving doctor
  • never deleted (by design)
A legal record in its own right, and personal data of staff.
Most sensitive · later

Patient Archive (MD2)

  • patient names, conditions, treatment
  • drugs given on a case (EDP F79)
Health data: the strictest rules in both countries. Not in release 1.

Also personal: the Activity log (who changed what, kept for good) and backups (a full copy of everything above). Each computer today keeps its own copy, unencrypted on its disk; the shared server (REACH SD2) is not decided yet.

The inventory screens you use every day

The June Inventory, rebuilt as a working demo for F6 overview, F7 Store and bags, F8 item page and F9 restock and dispense. The screens are the June ones. What's different is underneath. To see the difference, switch Rules to June at any time: the app then works out readiness the way the June app did.

You're signed in as Cara (Admin), so every button shows. All items and numbers are made up. Nothing is saved, except your answers in the right-hand column.

“Rules: June” only changes how readiness and stock levels are worked out, so you can compare the two. The new app has no such switch.

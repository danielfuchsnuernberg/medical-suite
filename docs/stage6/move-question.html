F8 lets you move all or part of a line to another bag or the Store. The checklist doesn't say what happens to the line you emptied. The demo you approved archived it. Built for now: it stays, at 0. Nothing is committed until you answer.

What it's about

A line in a bag isn't only stock: it also says what the bag must carry (its required amount). In the Store, it says when to reorder.

Why it matters

If the emptied line is archived, the bag forgets it needs Saline. Its card then says Ready with no Saline in it: the same kind of false “Ready” as the June bug B25. Someone grabs the bag for a MedEvac.

What each answer changes

Pick one: Bag 1's card and the Overview change with it. Lines with no required amount (like the Trauma kit's) are where the answers differ most.

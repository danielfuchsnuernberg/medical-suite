The questionThe total is still 5, so the balance doesn't change. But 2 are now in a bag that leaves the base. Does the register show that, and who has to confirm it?
What it's about

The register is the legal record of each controlled drug: every ampoule in, out or corrected, with who, a witness and the running balance. A move is different: nothing comes in or goes out, but the drug changes where it is.

Why it matters

If an ampoule goes missing from a bag, the register is where you look. If moves aren't in it, the register says “5, in the Store” while 2 are on a plane. If every move needs a doctor, stocking a bag before a flight waits for one.

What each answer changes

Pick one: the register and the item page on the left change with it. In every answer the move is also in Activity, and the balance stays 5.

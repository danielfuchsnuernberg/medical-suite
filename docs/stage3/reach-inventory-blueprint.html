Every June feature, and what happens to it

One list of people

Each person exists once, for every REACH app

You asked whether one app can still share one list of people with EDP. Yes. The list lives on the shared server, not inside either app. EDP plans cases and crews. The duty roster lives here, and EDP reads it to see who is on duty.

EDP owns

Cases and dispatch

Which case, which aircraft, who is assigned. When the dispatcher assigns a nurse or doctor, EDP checks this app's roster and only offers people on duty at that time.

Shared foundation

People, sign-in, activity log

One entry per person, with their qualifications (pilot, medic, nurse, …). One sign-in for every REACH app. Every change in every app goes into one log.

This app owns

Roster, items, stock, bags, training

The duty roster (which nurse or doctor works when), the item list, what's in the Store and in each bag, the controlled-drug register and staff training. EDP reads the roster for dispatch and the item list when a medic records medication.

Example: a new nurse joins example. You add her once. She can sign in to this app, you put her on the roster here, her training shows here, and EDP's dispatcher can assign her whenever she is on duty. Nobody types her name twice.

Controlled drugs

A full register: reason, witness, running balance (Q8)

For every controlled drug, every movement is one line that can't be changed afterwards. A mistake is fixed with a new correction line, so the history always adds up.
